## 2026.0.14

### Changed
- While you’re running a beta, “Get beta updates” in Settings stays on and can’t be turned off, so you always get the next beta. When you update to a full release, Locus Launcher asks whether you want to keep getting betas.

### Fixed
- New Window now works for Finder.
- Installing a beta now turns on beta updates. Before, a beta could miss the betas that came after it.

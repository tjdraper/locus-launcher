## 2026.0.6

### Added
- The launcher now lists apps with their names and icons (Sorting, keyboard selection and search come in future betas)
- Apps are found in Applications, the user's home folder Applications, and the system app folders, plus any other apps Spotlight knows about, including on external drives. Apps in Downloads, the Trash, Library folders, disk images, and inside other apps are left out.
- When the same app is installed in more than one place, it's listed once, preferring the copy in Applications.
- The list updates on its own when apps are installed, removed, or on a drive that's connected or ejected.

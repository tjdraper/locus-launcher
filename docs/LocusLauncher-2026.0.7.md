## 2026.0.7

### Added
- Apps in the launcher are grouped into alphabetical sections with letter headers. The current letter stays pinned at the top as you scroll. Apps whose names start with a number, a symbol, or a non-Latin letter are listed under # at the end.
- An A–Z index down the right side jumps to a letter. Click a letter or drag along the index. Dimmed letters have no apps and jump to the next letter that does.
- Use the up and down arrow keys to move the selection, and press Return to open the selected app. Clicking an app opens it, and moving the pointer over the list selects the app under it.
- If you close the launcher and open it again within 10 seconds, it picks up where you left off, with the same search text, cursor, selection and scroll position. Opening an app starts the launcher fresh next time.

### Changed
- Scrolling the app list is much smoother. Icons are prepared in the background, and the list no longer loads them as you scroll.

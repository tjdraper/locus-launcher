## 2026.0.12

### Added
- Hot keys and hidden apps with "Sync to other Macs" turned on now sync through iCloud to every Mac signed in to the same account. Adding, changing, or removing one on one Mac does the same on the others.
- If a hot key synced from another Mac uses keys this Mac already uses, for another hot key or the launcher, this Mac's keeps working. The synced one is outlined in red in its app's hot keys, with a message saying what uses the keys, and the App Hot Keys window marks it "keys in use".

### Changed
- Turning off "Sync to other Macs" keeps the hot key or hidden app on this Mac and removes it from your other Macs.
- Hot keys for apps that aren't on this Mac no longer take their keys, so those keys still work in other apps. They stay in the App Hot Keys window, marked "Not on this Mac".
- Choosing "Remind Me Later" in the update window, including after Check for Updates, now keeps the update sign in the menu bar and the launcher until you check again, so the update isn't forgotten.
